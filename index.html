A message from our founder

Giving back.
From the beginning.

Dear Friend,

From the first two dealerships Priority opened in 1999, our company has always made supporting local children’s charities a Priority. They’re our future, and over the years, we’ve contributed more than $3 million to over 35 different charities. Now, through our newly established Priority Automotive Charities, we can go even further, and support local children more than ever.

I hope you’ll make it a Priority to join one of our signature charity events as a sponsor or participant, or donate to one of these worthy Priority charities, and help us drive positive change in our community.

I hope to see you out there!

Sincerely, Dennis Ellmer
President & Founder, Priority Automotive

Draft notice · Updated September 25, 2026

This notice describes the current Priority Automotive Charities website preview. It will be updated as the final forms, donation services, and cookie controls are connected.

Information entered into preview forms

The contact, sponsorship, and beneficiary application forms are temporary previews. They do not submit information to Priority Automotive Charities or store it in this page’s browser storage. Fields clear when the pop-up closes. Document uploads are disabled.

Contacting us directly

Email and telephone links open your own email or phone application. Information you choose to send through those services is outside these preview forms. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information through the preview.

Donations and external services

The donation link takes you to an external payment service. That service handles information entered there under its own privacy terms. Social media links also open external websites with their own policies.

Cookies and website delivery

This page’s code does not currently install advertising cookies or analytics trackers. The website host may process technical information, such as an IP address and requested pages, to deliver and protect the site. Hosting practices are separate from the page’s preview controls.

The cookie preference pop-up is an interactive demonstration. Its toggle changes the displayed status only; it does not change actual cookies, record consent, or send an opt-out request. The demonstration resets when the page reloads.
