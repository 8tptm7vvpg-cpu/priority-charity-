A message from our founder

Giving back.
From the beginning.

Dear Friend,

From the first two dealerships Priority opened in 1999, our company has always made supporting local children’s charities a Priority. They’re our future, and over the years, we’ve contributed more than $3 million to over 35 different charities. Now, through our newly established Priority Automotive Charities, we can go even further, and support local children more than ever.

I hope you’ll make it a Priority to join one of our signature charity events as a sponsor or participant, or donate to one of these worthy Priority charities, and help us drive positive change in our community.

I hope to see you out there!

Sincerely, Dennis Ellmer
President & Founder, Priority Automotive
